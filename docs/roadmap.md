# Public roadmap

MarketLens v1.0.0 focuses on a complete, self-hostable local-market research
loop. The roadmap below is directional, not a commitment to a delivery date.

## v1.1 — Better comparisons

- Compare two saved research projects side by side.
- Save and switch between custom competition-scoring profiles.
- Improve density and opportunity visualizations.

## v1.2 — More data sources

- Package provider adapters as installable extensions.
- Add community-maintained Foursquare and Yelp adapters where terms permit.
- Add a deliberate CSV import provider with provenance labeling.

## v1.3 — Local-first AI

- Add Ollama and compatible local-model adapters.
- Support optional local embeddings for research notes.
- Improve offline report workflows.

## v1.4 — Research over time

- Persist historical snapshots and comparison views.
- Highlight changes in ratings, reviews, and business presence.
- Add opt-in scheduled research through a worker architecture.

## v2.0 — Collaborative operations

- Move long-running work to queues and dedicated workers.
- Add organizations, roles, and collaborative research workspaces.
- Explore a provider-plugin marketplace with clear compliance boundaries.

## How to influence the roadmap

Open a feature request with the decision it would improve, the data source or
workflow involved, and any provider terms that matter. Small, independently
useful pull requests are easier to evaluate and release than broad rewrites.
