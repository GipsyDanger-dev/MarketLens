# Design System — MarketLens

## Product Context

- **What this is:** A local market-research workspace that turns place data into explainable competitor intelligence.
- **Who it is for:** Entrepreneurs, researchers, consultants, and analysts who need evidence before making a local-market decision.
- **Project type:** Hybrid: editorial landing page with a data-dense research application.

## Aesthetic Direction

- **Direction:** Editorial data desk.
- **Decoration level:** Intentional. A fine survey grid, rule lines, and restrained tonal depth orient the eye without becoming ornament.
- **Mood:** Calm, investigative, and exact. It should feel closer to a well-edited field report than a generic SaaS dashboard.

## Typography

- **Display/Hero:** Fraunces — used for decisive editorial headlines and major figures.
- **Body/UI:** DM Sans — clear at small sizes, firm enough for controls and labels.
- **Data/Tables:** DM Sans with tabular numerals.
- **Code:** IBM Plex Mono.
- **Scale:** 12 / 14 / 16 / 20 / 26 / 36 / 52 / 72 px.

## Color

- **Approach:** Restrained. One mineral green accent against ink and parchment surfaces.
- **Ink:** `#16201B` — primary dark surface and text in inverse contexts.
- **Parchment:** `#F3F0E7` — default application background.
- **Paper:** `#FFFCF5` — raised workspace surface.
- **Accent:** `#476B50` — actions, data emphasis, and active states.
- **Accent light:** `#D5E0BF` — soft selection and chart fills.
- **Semantic:** success `#2F7A53`, warning `#A26825`, error `#A9463D`, info `#355F7A`.

## Spacing & Layout

- **Base unit:** 4px.
- **Density:** Comfortable around narrative content; compact inside tables and controls.
- **Grid:** 12 columns on wide screens, 6 on tablet, 1 on mobile.
- **Max content width:** 1280px.
- **Radius:** 4px for inputs, 8px for panels, 999px only for compact statuses.

## Motion

- **Approach:** Minimal-functional.
- **Timing:** 150ms for controls, 250ms for panels, 400ms only for entrance cues.
- **Rule:** Motion clarifies selection and progress. It never competes with data.
